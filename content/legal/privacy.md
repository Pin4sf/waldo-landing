## 3. Health information

Sharing health data with Waldo is optional, and you choose each source separately.

- **Sources.** With your permission, Waldo reads the health readings listed in section 2 from Apple Health on your iPhone. If we add other sources, we will update this policy first. Our servers receive summaries over short periods and single readings, not beat-to-beat heart data or your continuous heart-rate stream.
- **Two permissions, per source.** One lets Waldo store and process your readings. The other lets the AI that writes Waldo's replies use them. You can withdraw either at any time in the Waldo app. To turn on health sharing, you confirm that you are 18 or over.
- **How Waldo uses it.** To work out daily summaries and scores, and to plan around your sleep and your day, for example by suggesting a lighter morning after a short night. If you allow AI use, the model that writes Waldo's replies sees a short summary of your health in broad bands (such as "below your usual") with every reply and check-in, and can look up the readings it needs (section 6). When you finish setting up Waldo, and only if you allow AI use, the model also reads up to 30 days of your daily readings once, to describe a few patterns in words, without numbers, for your welcome picture (section 4). Waldo's memory may also hold health-related notes from your conversations, which you can ask it to forget.
- **Where it appears.** In Waldo's replies to you, in the app, the console or a channel you've linked. If you use Waldo on WhatsApp or Telegram, replies that mention your health pass through that service. Waldo doesn't put your health data into emails or messages to other people unless you ask it to.
- **What we never do with it.** We don't sell your health data, and we don't use or share it for advertising, marketing or data mining.
- **Beta diagnostics.** During the beta, our diagnostic records include the full text of every tester's conversations (section 10). Before a record is kept, Waldo removes the health readings it looked up and health numbers in the text, such as a heart rate, a blood pressure or hours of sleep. Health details you mention in words, such as a symptom or a medicine, can still be recorded.
- **How long we keep it.** Readings for up to 90 days, and daily summaries and scores for up to 24 months while your account is open. Withdrawing storage permission, or deleting your account, deletes them (section 10).

Waldo is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.

## 4. Connected accounts, your welcome picture and looking you up

You can connect your Google accounts and other apps to Waldo. Each service shows you the permissions Waldo asks for, and you decide whether to grant them.

**Google.** You can connect one or more Google accounts, and Waldo reads from each one you connect.

- **What Waldo can access.** With your permission: your calendar (events and free or busy times), Gmail (reading, drafting and sending email), Google Tasks, your saved contacts and the people you've emailed (read only), files in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides (read only), and the name and phone number on your Google profile.
- **How Waldo uses it.** Only for the features you use: answering your questions, preparing you for meetings, following up on email, planning your day, and the actions you approve (Terms of Use, section 5). For example, Waldo reads an email thread when you ask about it, and your calendar's busy time feeds your daily scores.
- **AI processing.** To answer you, Waldo sends the relevant part of this data, such as one email thread or the day's calendar, to the AI model provider that writes its reply (section 6). We don't use it to train AI models.
- **What we keep.** Waldo doesn't keep a copy of your mailbox or files. It keeps the results of its last few lookups so it can follow up, and anything quoted in your conversation stays in your history until you delete it. The tokens that let Waldo reach your Google account are kept in an encrypted vault.
- **Disconnecting.** Disconnect Google in the Waldo console, and we delete those tokens. To remove Waldo's access completely, also remove Waldo from your Google Account's third-party connections.

**Google API Services User Data Policy.** Waldo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we don't use Google user data for advertising, we don't sell it, we don't use it to train general AI or machine-learning models, and we don't transfer it except as that policy allows.

During the beta, our diagnostic records include the Google content Waldo read to answer you (section 10), and the Ecofresh team members who work on Waldo can see those records to find and fix problems. Accepting this policy in the Waldo app is your agreement to that. Apart from that, people at Ecofresh don't read your Google data except with your agreement, for security purposes such as investigating a bug or abuse, or to comply with the law.

**Other apps you connect.** In Connections, in the app or the console, you can connect apps such as Granola, Slack, Notion, Linear, Todoist and Wispr Flow, or another app by its address. You sign in with that app itself, and the sign-in Waldo receives is kept in an encrypted vault.

- **Reading.** Waldo can then use the app's reading tools whenever a reply or task needs them, for example to read your meeting notes before a meeting, or to search a Slack channel. Waldo never changes anything in Granola.
- **Changing or sending.** Tools that change something or send something, such as posting a Slack message as you or editing a Notion page, need your approval each time, unless you have allowed that tool to run without asking (Terms of Use, section 5). For each Slack message it sends, Waldo keeps a record of which channel it went to, not what it said.
- **AI processing.** Like other connected content, what Waldo reads from these apps goes to the AI model provider for that reply (section 6).
- **Disconnecting.** Disconnect the app in Connections, and Waldo asks the app to cancel that sign-in where the app supports it, and deletes it.

**Your welcome picture.** When you finish setting up Waldo in the app, it builds a first picture of you: a few short notes about your health, work, people, routines and interests, each showing the source it came from. To build it, Waldo reads each source once and sends it to the AI model provider in a request of its own (section 6):

- **Health**, only if you share it and allow AI use: up to 30 days of your daily readings.
- **Calendar and Google Tasks:** the titles, times and repeats of your events from the last 60 days and the next 14, how many people each event has, and your tasks. Not event descriptions, and not who the attendees are.
- **Email:** for the last 90 days, leaving out promotions and social mail, who each email is from (name and domain), its subject line and its date. Never the body of an email.

Waldo keeps only the notes the model writes, in words and without numbers, and pointers to the events and email threads they rest on. You can confirm, correct or forget each note in the app, and a note you forget or correct isn't added again. Things Waldo found on the public web appear only as look-up findings (below).

**Learning as your day goes.** After your welcome picture, Waldo keeps its notes about you up to date. A few times a day, and when new email or calendar changes arrive, Waldo's model looks over your recent emails (including their text), calendar events (including their descriptions and who is invited) and conversations, and may note a fact or pattern that would help it help you, such as a project you're running, someone you meet often or a bill that's due. A note learned this way is marked as Waldo's read, and Waldo doesn't treat it as something you said until you confirm it. You can confirm, correct or forget each note, and a note you forget or correct isn't added again. Waldo still doesn't keep a copy of your mailbox.

**Finding the right note.** So Waldo can find a note when you ask about it in different words, it turns each memory note into an embedding: a list of numbers that captures what the note means. Cloudflare's AI service, which already runs Waldo's servers, computes it. To search, it does the same for the message you're asking with; that one isn't stored. A note's embedding is stored with your memory and deleted with the note or your account. Health readings are left out of what is sent, and none of it is used to train AI models.

**Looking you up on the public web (only if you say yes).** If you say yes in the Waldo app, Waldo may look you up on the public web to learn about your work and public life, so it can help you better. It searches using your name and the handles, sites and email addresses you have given it, through Brave Search, which receives those search terms. It reads the public pages it finds, and those pages go to Waldo's AI model providers as part of the conversation (section 6).

Waldo keeps only facts about you that a page shows plainly, such as your work, education, public work, where you say you are based, public profiles, interests and achievements. It is built not to record your health, religion, caste, politics or sexuality from these pages, and it doesn't look up other people. Everything it finds is listed in What Waldo knows with the page it came from, and stays unconfirmed until you keep, correct or forget it.

A finding you leave unconfirmed for 30 days is removed from What Waldo knows; anything Waldo already quoted, read or noted about it stays in your chats and in Waldo's notes on past conversations. You can take this yes back at any time in the app: Waldo then deletes every finding still listed and the passages it quoted, and doesn't record those same items again. Waldo's own replies about them stay in that chat until you delete it. If your yes stops counting without you taking it back, for example because this policy changed and you haven't accepted the new version yet, what Waldo found stays listed and Waldo can still use it, so it can still reach Waldo's AI model providers, until you forget it or take back your yes.

## 5. Messaging channels

## 6. How Waldo uses AI

Waldo is an AI assistant. Its replies are written by AI models, and no person reviews them before you see them.

To answer you, Waldo sends an AI model provider what it needs for that reply: your message, recent conversation, relevant things Waldo remembers about you, and, where they help and you've allowed it, your health data and content from your connected accounts.

- **Replies:** OpenAI's models write Waldo's replies and do supporting work such as titling threads, updating memory and building your welcome picture. When OpenAI can't answer, Anthropic's models answer instead.
- **Voice notes:** transcribed into text by ElevenLabs, smallest.ai or OpenAI.
- **Web search:** your search queries go to OpenAI's web search or Brave Search, with your approximate location (your country, or your time zone if you're outside India) so results are local. If you said yes to being looked up, Brave Search also receives the searches about you (section 4).
- **Websites:** when Waldo looks at or fills in a website for you, a browser service (Cloudflare or Browserbase) loads the page, and an AI model reads what's on it.

These providers process the data to provide their service to us. Under their terms for business use, OpenAI and Anthropic don't use what we send them to train their models. OpenAI keeps records of requests for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, and longer only where the law requires it or to prevent harm.

**Reaching a person.** You can reach someone at Ecofresh any time at woof@heywaldo.in.

## 12. How we protect your data

- Data travels between you, Waldo and our providers over encrypted connections, and our storage providers encrypt the data they store.
- You don't have a Waldo password. You sign in with one-time codes, or with Apple or Google. The app keeps you signed in for 30 days at a time; the console signs you out after 7 days without use, and 30 days after you sign in at most. You can sign a device out at any time.
- Health data is stored separately, and the database checks every request to read or change it.
- Tokens for your connected accounts are kept in an encrypted vault.
- Waldo checks that messages from WhatsApp, Telegram and Google really come from them before acting on them.
- Waldo asks for your approval before it sends an email, changes your calendar or Google Tasks, submits a form on a website, runs a command on your Mac, or changes or sends something in another app you connect, unless you have allowed that action to run without asking (Terms of Use, section 5).
- We limit repeated sign-in attempts and other requests, to protect accounts from abuse.
- Only the Ecofresh team members who run, support or secure Waldo can access personal data, and only when they need to.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you, and the authorities, as the law requires.
