# What we still need to explain about your data

The app privacy policy and provider disclosures are not published yet. We should not describe intended boundaries as verified controls.

Before asking you to connect sensitive accounts, the product needs to explain what each permission allows, whether it reads message content or metadata, where data is stored, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how access or deletion requests work.

The website uses sample data to illustrate workflows. It does not establish that a particular privacy control is shipped. No encryption, on-device storage, training exclusion or universal undo guarantee is made here.

A change may or may not be reversible depending on the connected service. The product should explain those limits before asking you to approve it.

See the [current privacy notice](/privacy) for the limited website waitlist information that is documented. Final app terms are still pending.
